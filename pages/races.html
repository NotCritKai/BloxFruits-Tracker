How to obtain races and gears

Human, Shark, Rabbit, and Angel can be starting races or obtained by rerolling with Tort for 3,000 Fragments or through the shop.

For races other than Draco, V2 requires the Alchemist's flower quest at level 850, the Colosseum quest, and $500,000. V3 requires V2, level 1000, Don Swan, Arowe's race quest, and $2,000,000.

For V4, the other races need the Temple of Time puzzle, Mirage Island's Blue Gear, and a full-moon trial with at least three different races. Win the trial to unlock awakening; later trials and training unlock the remaining gears.

The first yellow gear unlocks V4. The next three slots are red or blue: each color can be selected up to twice, with a maximum of three ability gears total. The final yellow gear improves awakening duration and energy gain.

Sources: races, gears, abilities, and Draco embers.

Human

Obtain: Possible starting race; also available from race rerolls.

V3: Defeat Diamond, Jeremy, and Orbitus. V4: Complete the Trial of Strength.

Selected Human gears

Tier 1 builds rage to increase power. Tier 2 extends the rage duration and raises its limit.

Tier 1 adds Flash Step charges, brief invisibility, and speed. Tier 2 improves the dash and charge recovery and prevents interruption by damage.

Fishman (Shark)

Obtain: Possible starting race; also available from race rerolls.

V3: Defeat a naturally spawned Sea Beast. V4: Complete the Trial of Water.

Selected Shark gears

Tier 1 applies a stacking slow on attacks. Tier 2 makes the slow stronger and longer.

Tier 1 grants a shield replenished by dealing damage. Tier 2 increases its capacity and recovery.

Mink (Rabbit)

Obtain: Possible starting race; also available from race rerolls.

V3: Collect 30 chests. V4: Complete the Trial of Speed.

Selected Rabbit gears

Tier 1 creates stunning tornadoes when dashing. Tier 2 improves their range, damage, and stun.

Tier 1 extends dash distance. Tier 2 adds a super dash when the dash button is held.

Sky Islander (Angel)

Obtain: Possible starting race; also available from race rerolls.

V3: Defeat another Angel player. V4: Complete the Trial of the King.

Selected Angel gears

Tier 1 creates an aura that slows, damages, drains energy, and distorts enemies' vision. Tier 2 strengthens these effects.

Tier 1 enables gliding and flight. Tier 2 improves their speed and effect.

Ghoul

Obtain: At level 1000, give Experimic 100 Ectoplasm and a Hellfire Torch on the Cursed Ship. After unlocking it, switching back is free.

V3: Defeat five players. V4: Complete the Trial of Carnage.

Selected Ghoul gears

Tier 1 creates a dark field that weakens enemies and enables night passives. Tier 2 expands it and adds attacking crows.

Tier 1 grants life leech on attacks. Tier 2 increases the healing.

Android (Cyborg)

Obtain: Insert a Fist of Darkness at the Hot and Cold laboratory, obtain Order's Core Brain, and pay the secret NPC 2,500 Fragments. Re-equipping also costs 2,500 Fragments.

V3: Show Arowe a physical fruit. V4: Complete the Trial of the Machine.

Selected Cyborg gears

Tier 1 applies an electrical effect that drains Instinct dodges. Tier 2 makes the effect stronger and longer.

Tier 1 chains damage through orbs and enables super jumps with Instinct. Tier 2 strengthens the orbs and removes the Instinct requirement.

Dragon (Draco)

Obtain: Get 500 Dragon Talon mastery and all Dojo Belts, learn Dragon Tether, then bring a Dragon Egg from the Prehistoric Island to the Dragon Wizard. Switching back is free.

V2: At level 2000, bring the Dragon Wizard five Fire Flowers and $1,000,000. V3: Complete the Horned Man quest, deal at least 10% damage to a defeated Terrorshark, and pay the Dragon Wizard $3,000,000.

V4: Reach 500 mastery on Dragon Talon, Dragonheart, and Dragonstorm; freeze Hydra Island with a Leviathan Heart, open the Dojo Vault, and complete the Volcano Event and Trial of Flames. It does not require the Mirage Blue Gear, a full moon, or other players.

Draco chooses embers at the Flames of Hearth. V4 also improves Fiery Flight. The first and final embers are yellow; the three ability embers follow the same red/blue limits as gears.

Selected Draco embers

Tier 1 marks enemies with explosive orbs and boosts nearby allies' damage. Tier 2 increases the explosions' damage.

Tier 1 reflects incoming damage and improves nearby allies' defense. Tier 2 strengthens both effects.
